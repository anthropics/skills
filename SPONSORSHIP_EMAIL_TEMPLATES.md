## Email Writing Tips for Maximum Response Rate

### Subject Line Strategy:
- **DO:** Personalize with brand name ("Partnership with Nike")
- **DO:** Include follower count if impressive (50K+)
- **DON'T:** Use generic subject lines ("Brand Collaboration")
- **Best Practice:** Reference specific product/campaign they're running

### Body Content:
- **Lead with metrics** that matter to brands (engagement rate beats follower count)
- **Be specific** about what you'll create (3 Reels, not "content")
- **Show audience alignment** (why THEIR customers follow YOU)
- **Include example links** to best-performing content if possible
- **Give clear CTAs** (link to media kit, calendar link, phone number)

### Personalization Checklist:
- [ ] Research brand's current campaign
- [ ] Reference specific product(s) you love
- [ ] Mention why audience is perfect fit
- [ ] Check spelling of brand name and recipient name
- [ ] Use brand's tone (corporate vs. casual)
- [ ] Include working contact information
- [ ] Attach media kit (optional but professional)

### Optimal Send Times:
- **Tuesday-Thursday** (brands read business emails mid-week)
- **9-10 AM or 2-3 PM** (before lunch or afternoon coffee)
- **Avoid Fridays** (overlooked in weekend inbox shuffle)

### Follow-Up Strategy:
- **Day 5**: No response? Send friendly follow-up
- **Day 10**: Final follow-up with urgency ("launching campaign soon")
- **Day 14**: Move on to next prospect

### Response Tracking:
- Copy all brand emails into spreadsheet
- Track: Send date, Brand name, Email address, Response date, Status
- Note: Which brands respond fastest (prioritize similar brands)
- Analyze: Which subject lines get best open rates

## Pro Tips for Closing Deals

1. **Price Anchoring:** Start at 20-30% higher than your target rate
2. **Bundle Discounts:** Offer 15% off for 3+ Reels
3. **Performance Guarantees:** "If post gets under [X] views, discount applied"
4. **First-Time Discount:** "New partnership rate: 20% off for first collaboration"
5. **Faster Turnaround = Higher Rate:** "48-hour delivery: +₹2,000 rush fee"
6. **Exclusivity:** "Will not promote competitor for 30 days: +₹1,000"

## After Deal Confirmed

Once brand agrees:

1. **Send Contract/SOW** (Statement of Work):
   - Deliverables (exactly what content pieces)
   - Timeline (dates for delivery)
   - Payment terms (50% upfront, 50% on delivery)
   - Revision rounds (usually 1-2 free revisions)
   - Usage rights (posting dates, how long content stays up)

2. **Request Brand Guidelines:**
   - Product images/colors they prefer
   - Key messaging points
   - Hashtags to include
   - Link/discount codes to feature

3. **Create Project Plan:**
   - Content calendar (which days posting)
   - Filming dates
   - Editing timeline
   - Story posting sequence

4. **Send Updates:**
   - Progress check-ins
   - Content preview before posting (24 hours prior)
   - Analytics after posting (reach, engagement, clicks)

5. **Request Testimonial/Future Work:**
   - "How did this collaboration perform?"
   - "Open to ongoing monthly partnership?"
   - "Can I feature your testimonial for other brands?"

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ Brands asking for free "exposure"
❌ Vague contracts with unlimited revisions
❌ Brands requesting login credentials or account access
❌ Requests to delete/hide content after posting
❌ Extremely low rates (don't devalue your work)
❌ Brands with negative/fake reviews online
❌ Requesting payment before contract signing

✅ Always get agreement in writing
✅ Set clear revision limits (usually 2)
✅ Require 50% payment upfront minimum
✅ Own your content (brand can't repurpose)
✅ Post at optimal times for YOUR audience
