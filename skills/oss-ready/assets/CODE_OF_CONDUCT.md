# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want {{PROJECT_NAME}} to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
experience level, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give constructive feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat)
and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact
{{CONDUCT_CONTACT}}. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so
repeatedly or seriously.
