## EXECUTION PIPELINE & OUTPUT SCHEMA

- Synthesize a formal aesthetic movement specification (`DesignPhilosophy`) from `UserInput` parameters in Stage 1 and export the specification as a `.md` file.
- Render a static visual composition (`CanvasArtifact`) from `DesignPhilosophy` and `UserInput` parameters in Stage 2 and export the artifact as a `.pdf` or `.png` file.

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY SPECIFICATION

### MOVEMENT IDENTIFIER & STRUCTURAL AXES

- Define an aesthetic movement identifier consisting of 1 to 2 words that encapsulates the primary geometric and chromatic tension.
- Construct the `DesignPhilosophy` specification in 4 to 6 paragraphs, allocating 1 dedicated analysis per structural dimension across 5 orthogonal visual axes, separate from document layout templates or explanatory prose blocks:
  + Space and Form ➔ Coordinate division, positive-to-negative surface ratio, and geometric or organic boundary contours.
  + Color and Material ➔ Primary chromatic zones, tonal contrast intervals, and surface texture properties.
  + Scale and Rhythm ➔ Proportional size ratios between structural elements and repetition intervals across the canvas plane.
  + Composition and Balance ➔ Focal weight distribution, symmetry or asymmetry axes, and directional vector tension.
  + Visual Hierarchy ➔ Reading sequence across primary geometric forms, secondary structural markers, and tertiary typographic labels.

### FORMAL SPECIFICATION PARAMETERS

- Allocate canvas surface coverage at a 90:10 ratio between visual composition elements (`90%` geometric forms, chromatic fields, patterns, and imagery) and typographic accents (`10%` structural labels and indexical markers).
- Declare each structural rule at a single authoritative locus within the `.md` specification, maintaining semantic orthogonality across the 5 visual axes.
- Formulate the aesthetic framework using domain-independent geometric and chromatic laws, separate from literal subject descriptions in `UserInput`.
- Specify execution precision through measurable geometric parameters: orthogonal grid alignment, calibrated kerning intervals, uniform stroke weights, and balanced negative-space distribution.
- Calibrate aesthetic movement archetypes according to two-sided structural mappings (`Movement Archetype ➔ Target Visual & Spatial Specification`):
  + Concrete Poetry ➔ Monumental geometric blocks, high-contrast Brutalist spatial partitions, sculptural display glyphs paired with micro-scale index labels, and asymmetric weight tension.
  + Chromatic Language ➔ Calibrated color-field zones encoding primary relational data, Josef Albers-style chromatic interaction, and micro sans-serif coordinate anchors.
  + Analog Meditation ➔ Tactile paper-grain textures, controlled ink-bleed gradients, expansive negative space exceeding 60% of the canvas area, and low-contrast peripheral typographic captions.
  + Organic Systems ➔ Modular biomorphic clustering, curvilinear growth trajectories, earth-to-architectural tonal palettes, and relational node diagrams with floating index labels.
  + Geometric Silence ➔ Swiss modular grid divisions, high-contrast monochromatic or duotone planes, orthogonal axis alignment, and restrained typographic coordinates adjacent to open spatial zones.

## CONCEPTUAL MOTIF ENCODING

- Extract the core thematic concept from `UserInput` and encode it into `CanvasArtifact` as an implicit structural motif.
- Map domain-specific attributes from `UserInput` onto abstract geometric proportions, rhythmic intervals, and chromatic ratios, separate from literal illustration or explicit textual narration.
- Integrate the extracted conceptual motif into the coordinate architecture defined by `DesignPhilosophy`:
  + Narrative or entertainment domain input ➔ Structural proportions, symbolic geometric primitives, and tonal atmosphere derived from the source thematic structure, separate from figurative caricature.
  + Technical or analytical domain input ➔ Systematic reference markers, coordinate indices, layered pattern arrays, and diagrammatic vector paths.

## CANVAS COMPOSITION & TYPOGRAPHY

### GEOMETRIC & CHROMATIC CONSTRUCTION

- Construct a 1-page visual composition in `.pdf` or `.png` format as the default output unit, or generate `N` sequential pages when multi-page output is specified in `UserInput`.
- Restrict the active color palette to 2 to 4 core chromatic values combined with neutral ground tones.
- Build visual density through geometric primitives, mathematically aligned repeating patterns, layered vector paths, and systematic coordinate markers.

### TYPOGRAPHIC INTEGRATION & FONT LOADING

- Load typeface files from the `./canvas-fonts` directory and assign distinct font families across typographic hierarchy levels.
- Calibrate typographic stroke weight and glyph scale according to the domain attributes of `UserInput`:
  + High-energy or expressive domain context ➔ High-weight display geometry (`700–900` weight class) and macro-scale typographic anchors integrated into the primary composition grid.
  + Contemplative or minimalist domain context ➔ Light-weight stroke geometry (`100–300` weight class) and micro-scale indexical labels positioned at grid intersections.
- Place typography directly onto the canvas coordinate plane as an integrated geometric component.

### MULTI-PAGE SERIALIZATION

- Generate each subsequent page in a multi-page sequence as a distinct structural variation derived from the active `DesignPhilosophy` parameters.
- Preserve chromatic palette continuity and grid modulus across each page while varying focal geometry, scale ratios, and spatial density per page.
- Compile multi-page outputs into a single `.pdf` document or an indexed sequence of `.png` files.

## SPATIAL & QUALITY VERIFICATION

- Enforce a minimum perimeter margin clearance of 5% to 10% of each canvas axis across the 4 outer boundaries, positioning each graphic and typographic bounding box inside the active viewport.
- Maintain a 0px bounding-box intersection tolerance between distinct text labels, coordinate markers, and primary geometric forms, preserving measurable negative-space separation between adjacent elements.
- Refine visual cohesion during the pre-export code audit by recalibrating coordinate alignment, stroke proportions, and chromatic contrast of existing canvas elements, separate from appending extra decorative shapes or post-processing filters.
