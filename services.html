How engagements take shape ↗

Services shaped around your organisation.

Reviews can focus on a specific function or connect several areas, depending on your priorities and the scope agreed with management.

Governance & assurance

  • Internal controls & risk management

    Review how the organisation safeguards its assets, prevents errors and fraud, assigns responsibilities and manages key risks.

  • Accounting systems review

    Assess transaction recording, reconciliations, financial controls and the reliability of accounting information.

  • Corporate governance

    Review oversight, accountability, policies, decision making and reporting to directors or other governing bodies.

Financial & management insight

  • Financial performance review

    Analyse revenue, costs, profitability, cash flow and budgets to help management understand results and take action.

  • IT systems integration & performance

    Assess whether operational and accounting systems work effectively together and produce complete, reliable information.

  • Management reporting & executive dashboards

    Help develop clear, timely reports that highlight performance, exceptions and decisions requiring attention.

Supply & operations

  • Procurement management

    Review purchasing procedures, supplier selection, approvals, receipt of goods and payments.

  • Inventory management

    Assess stock records, movements, physical counts, valuation, wastage and controls over stores.

  • Operational efficiency

    Review workflows and service delivery to identify delays, duplication, avoidable costs and opportunities for improvement.

People & growth

  • Marketing & business growth

    Review marketing activities, customer development and opportunities to increase sales.

  • Human resources & productivity

    Review roles, staffing practices, performance management and the effective use of employees' time and skills.

  • Staff training

    Provide practical training in controls, procedures, reporting and other areas identified during the engagement.
